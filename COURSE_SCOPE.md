# Hanami V15 — scope, content inventory and honesty policy

## Content now bundled

- **5 JLPT-inspired tiers, 20 Hanami steps per tier**: N5, N4, N3, N2, N1 (100 steps).
- **375 distinct classroom grammar lessons** (75 per level), curated and explained, with 750 Japanese examples and 750 English equivalents.
- **75 authored chapter reading passages**, each with two authored questions.
- **30 original anime-style conversations** and **25 original real-world dialogue lessons**, with controlled comprehension checks and self-directed writing.
- **16 Kana Bootcamp lessons** for new learners, and a small preinstalled vocab/kanji set. The greater OpenJLPT CC BY-SA 4.0 content is downloadable, not packaged.

## What “complete” means in the Level 1–100 UI

- **Stage cleared:** completed one controlled question set (at least 80% first-try correctness, including a reading and an audio-dependent or explicitly reading-only question), and entered a Japanese sentence. A stage can be passed via reading-only mode if speech is unsupported; this does *not* create listening evidence.
- **Audio practice:** Japanese speech playback was requested on a supported device or through a matching licensed imported clip. Browser TTS playback success is not guaranteed by this indicator.
- **Retained:** self-rated successful recall on three different calendar dates spanning at least six days. It is not independently graded output.
- **JLPT N1:** Hanami does not claim certification, full vocabulary coverage, fluent speech, or an accurate exam score.
